Sacred places within our premises

The Maulay Devi Mandir, within our premises, has a long history. Shirdi villagers, especially newly-weds, and Shirdi residents come here regularly to seek blessings and make offerings on important occasions. The Shri Datta murti stands just opposite the mandir.

Questions

Staying near the Samadhi Mandir: common questions

How far is Hotel Saikrupa from Shri Saibaba Samadhi Mandir?

350 m on foot, about 5 minutes, to Gate 5/6 of the Samadhi Mandir. The hotel is behind the Shirdi Nagar Parishad office on Kankuri Road.

Do you have family rooms for 4 to 6 people?

Yes. We have family rooms with 2 double beds and family suites with 2 rooms behind one private entrance, each for up to 6 guests.

Do you have AC and non-AC rooms?

Yes. Doubles, triples and family rooms are available in both AC and non-AC.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is at 12 noon and check-out at 11 am.

Is parking available?

Yes, there is ample parking for cars and buses at the hotel.

How far is Sainagar Shirdi railway station and Shirdi Airport?

Sainagar Shirdi railway station is about 3 km away and Shirdi Airport about 14 km. We can help arrange a taxi.

How do I pay the advance?

Pay online with the “Pay advance online” buttons on this page, by UPI or card. The advance is Rs 500 for a total bill up to Rs 1,500, Rs 1,000 for Rs 1,501 to Rs 3,000, and Rs 1,500 above Rs 3,000, with GST included. You pay the rest at the hotel. Then send the payment confirmation on WhatsApp so we can confirm your room.

Do your prices go up at festivals?

Festival nights (Shri Saibaba Punyatithi 18-10-2026 to 21-10-2026, Diwali 05-11-2026 to 11-11-2026, Republic Day 23-01-2027 to 25-01-2027) use our festival price; the Diwali holidays (12-11-2026 to 15-11-2026) and Makar Sankranti (13-01-2027 to 15-01-2027) our Saturday price; and Christmas–New Year (23-12-2026 to 04-01-2027) our published tariff. Since 1988 we have never charged above our published tariff.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Cancellation is free until 12 noon, one day before your check-in date. You can choose either a full refund of your advance, or keep the advance as credit for a stay within 6 months. After that, or if you do not arrive, the first night is charged. On festival dates, free cancellation is until 7 days before check-in.

How do I get the best price?

Book directly on WhatsApp at 8262 800 200. Our direct prices are always below booking sites.
